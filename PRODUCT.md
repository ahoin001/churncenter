# ChurnCenter

## What it is

A calm headquarters for bank checking/savings bonus chasing: watch deals, enroll, track typed requirements (direct deposit, spend, balance holds), collect payouts, close cleanly, and resurface when you are re-eligible.

## Who it is for

People who churn bank account bonuses and currently track everything in spreadsheets, notes, or memory — and feel stressed by deadlines, cooldowns, and capital tied up.

## Job to be done

At a glance: what needs action, what is blocking payout, how much is expected vs locked, and when a bank becomes new-customer eligible again.

## Experience principles

1. **Money place, not cockpit** — one clear story per viewport; atmosphere before widgets.
2. **Calm clarity** — Robinhood-scale numbers, Uber-clean chrome, Companion soft materials.
3. **Follow published terms** — tracker and coach, never fake-DD guidance.
4. **Local-first** — persistence on device through V3; export/import for safety.
5. **Systems over one-offs** — tokens, motion catalog, and domain engines own the feel and math.

## Non-goals (through V3)

- Bank account aggregation / Plaid
- Accounts, sync, or multi-device cloud auth
- Public deal marketplace / social feed
- Coaching users to misrepresent qualifying activity

## Success

A user opens Today on payday and knows exactly what to move, what to wait on, and what unlocks soon — without feeling hunted by the UI.
